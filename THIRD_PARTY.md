# Third-party material

`vendor/so101/` is copied from `TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100`, commit
`eecbe3e0a9ebb23e25ad7b2759b03884c6660903`, under Apache-2.0. The original
license and notice are retained beside the files. The project uses the upstream
SO-101 MuJoCo model and meshes; the dual-arm scene, planner schema, tests, and
deployment scaffold are new work in this repository.

Candidate hackathon submissions found during research were inspected only to
understand feasibility. Their source code is not incorporated here.
